About the reconstruction

The workshop and covered-space dimensions use TINKERSPACE.pdf. The adjoining-room layout follows the earlier screenshot, as corrected. Unlabelled wall thicknesses, passage offsets and stair dimensions remain estimates. Roof height and grounds use the photographs.

Live makers appear as generic avatars. Names reflect active check-ins; positions and motion are simulated.
